# Latin verbs

## Conjugations

There are 4 conjugations in Latin. explaining them simply,

<!--| Infinitive | -āre | -ēre | -ĕre | -īre |-->
| Form | I | II | III | IV |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Present stem | -ā | -ē | -ĕ | -ī |
| Verb endings | -a- | -e- | -ĭ- | -ī- |

To find the present stem of the verb you should take -re off the infinitive form:
- amāre -> amā; ends in **-ā**, so I conjugation.

## Tenses

The continuous forms and passives are not listed here.

### Præsens actīvī (Present active)

Basically to conjugate easily you only need to remember:

- the endings (in the table below)
- II and IV 1st singular is **-io** not just **-o** (like I and III)
- -unt for III and IV conjugation 3rd plural


| Person | Singular | Plural |
| -- | -- | -- |
| 1st | -ō | -mus |
| 2nd | -s | -tis |
| 3rd | -t | -nt |

Let's look at what we derive from it:

| Conjugation | 1st singular | 2nd singular | 3rd singular | 1st plural | 2nd plural | 3rd plural |
| -- | -- | -- | -- | -- | -- | -- |
| I | am**ō** | amā**mus** | amā**s** | amā**tis** | ama**t** | ama**nt** |
| II | doc*ē***o** | docē**mus** | docē**s** | docē**tis** | doce**t** | doce**nt** |
| III | scrīb**o** | scrībĭ**mus** | scrībi**s** | scrībĭ**tis** | 3rd | scrībi**t** | scrību**nt** |
| IV | sci**o** | scī**mus** | scī**s** | scī**tis** | scī**t** | scī**nt** |
