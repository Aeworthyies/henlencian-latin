# Henlencian Latin nouns

## Cases

There are 5 cases (no vocatīvus). They correspond with Russian.

| Case | Question | English example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nominatīvus | what? who? (is) | **He** is here.
| Genitīvus | (...) of what? of whom? | It's **his**.
| Datīvus | (give) what? whom? | Give **him** a pen.
| Accusatīvus | (accuse) what? whom? | Give him **a pen**.
| Ablatīvus | (talk) about what? about who? AND with what? with whom? | We don't talk **about him**.

## Declentions

There are 5 declentions in Classical Latin, but in Henlencian &mdash; 3. They are:

| Declention | When used | Genitīvus | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | when noun ends in -a | -æ | Henlencia |
| 2 | when noun ends in -us, -um, -er | -i | lupus |
| 3 | when noun ends in consonants other than said above | -is | lex |

### 1st declention

| Case | Singular | Plural |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nominatīvus | Henlenci**a** | Henlenci**æ** |
| Genitīvus | Henlenci**æ** | Henlenci**ārum** |
| Datīvus | Henlenci**æ** | Henlenci**is** |
| Accusatīvus | Henlenci**am** | Henlenci**as** |
| Ablatīvus | Henlenci**ā** | Henlenci**is** |

### 2nd declention

| Case | Singular | Plural |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nominatīvus | lup**us** | lup**i** |
| Genitīvus | lup**i** | lup**ōrum** |
| Datīvus | lup**o** | lup**is** |
| Accusatīvus | lup**um** | lup**os** |
| Ablatīvus | lup**o** | lup**is** |

### 3rd declention

| Case | Singular | Plural |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nominatīvus | princip**s** | princip**es** |
| Genitīvus | princip**is** | princip**um** |
| Datīvus | princip**i** | princip**ībus** |
| Accusatīvus | princip**em** | princip**es** |
| Ablatīvus | princip**e** | princip**ībus** |
