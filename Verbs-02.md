## Tenses

### Future tense

For the future tense we split out 4 conjugations into 2 groups (1st group &mdash; I and II; 2nd group &mdash; III and IV).

#### 1st group (I and II)

For this group we take the present stem and add these endings:

| Person | Singular | Plural |
| -- | -- | -- |
| 1st | -bō | -bimus |
| 2nd | -bis | -bitis |
| 3rd | -bit | -bunt |

| Conjugation | 1st singular | 1st plural | 2nd singular | 2nd plural | 3rd singular | 3rd plural |
| -- | -- | -- | -- | -- | -- | -- |
| I | amā**bō** | amā**bimus** | amā**bis** | amā**bitis** | amā**bit** | amā**bunt** |
| II | docē**bō** | docē**bimus** | docē**bis** | docē**bitis** | docē**bit** | docē**bunt** |

#### 2nd group (III and IV)

For this one we take the present stem and add these endings:

| Person | Singular | Plural |
| -- | -- | -- |
| 1st | -am | -ēmus |
| 2nd | -ēs | -ētis |
| 3rd | -et | -ent |

Note: override the -ĕ at the end of the III conjugation stem.


| Conjugation | 1st singular | 1st plural | 2nd singular | 2nd plural | 3rd singular | 3rd plural |
| -- | -- | -- | -- | -- | -- | -- |
| III | scrīb**ām** | scrīb**ēmus** | scrīb**ēs** | scrīb**ētis** | scrīb**ēt** | scrīb**ēnt** |
| IV | sci**ām** | sci**ēmus** | sci**ēs** | sci**ētis** | sci**ēt** | sci**ēnt** |
